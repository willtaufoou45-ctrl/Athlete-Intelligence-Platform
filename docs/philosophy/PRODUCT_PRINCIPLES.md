# Product Principles

## 1. Coaching Comes First

Technology should support coaching, not replace it.
If testing isn’t fast, simple, and actionable, it will never become part of a coach’s workflow.

---

## 2. Objective Testing Drives Decisions

Testing removes guesswork and provides objective evidence for coaching decisions.
Objective testing identifies strengths and weaknesses.

Training should primarily focus on the athlete’s greatest limitation.
---

## 3. AIP Recommends. The Coach Decides.

AIP provides evidence-based recommendations while preserving coach autonomy.
Insights directing focus

---

## 4. Simple Wins

Coaches should receive only the information they need at the moment they need it.
One coaching cue or area of focus to change that session

---

## 5. Training Blocks Drive Development

Coaching decisions are made over weeks, not individual workouts.
It takes time to change 

---

## 6. Diagnose Before You Prescribe

Never prescribe training before identifying the athlete's primary limitation.

---

## 7. Every Test Should Lead to Action

If a measurement does not influence a coaching decision, it should not be collected.

---

## 8. Technology Should Be Invisible

Coaches should think in coaching language, not technology language.

---

## 9. Every Assessment Should Teach

Testing should educate both the coach and the athlete.

---

## 10. Build for Every Coach

Beginner coaches receive guidance.

Experienced coaches receive insights.
