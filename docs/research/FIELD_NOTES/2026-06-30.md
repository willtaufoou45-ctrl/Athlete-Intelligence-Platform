# Field Notes

**Date:** June 30, 2026

## Session Overview

- 6 athletes
- 7th–8th grade
- Day 1
- Focus: Acceleration + Lower Body Lift

---

## Observations

### Parent Engagement

Two parents stayed for the session.

One parent listened throughout the workout and wanted to understand the purpose behind each drill.

The second parent asked what their athlete should work on outside of training. We discussed sprint mechanics, force production, and ground contact for 5–10 minutes.

**Insight**

Parents don't just want results. They want education and confidence that the training has a purpose.

---

### Sprint Testing

The parent volunteered to record sprint times.

This allowed me to focus on coaching instead of holding the timing gate remote, phone, and recording data simultaneously.

We completed approximately 5–6 sprint reps in about 10 minutes.

**Insight**

The bottleneck isn't timing gates.

The bottleneck is recording data while coaching.

---

### Video

Watching athletes reminded me why video is important.

Video replay acts like a second coach.

Athletes understand coaching cues much faster when they immediately see themselves.

**Insight**

Video isn't just analysis.

It's an immediate coaching feedback tool.

---

### Weight Room

Force plate testing created a bottleneck.

While testing two athletes, the rest of the group became uncertain about what to do next.

The workout slowed because I was unavailable to lead the room.

**Insight**

The workflow should continue even when the coach is occupied.

The software should help direct athletes independently.

---

### Parent Follow-up

Parent text:

> "...how do Hudson and Bryson's force metrics compare..."

The parent wasn't asking for numbers.

They were asking for interpretation.

**Insight**

Parents care less about the metrics and more about understanding what the metrics mean and how they relate to their child's performance.

---

## Workflow Bottlenecks

- Recording sprint times while coaching
- Force plate testing pauses the workout
- Athletes rely on coach direction between stations
- Parent conversations reduce available lifting time

---

## Product Opportunities

- Athlete queue during sprint testing
- Automatic sprint recording
- Immediate video replay after each sprint
- Guided force plate testing
- Athlete station instructions while coach is busy
- Parent-friendly explanation of force plate results
- Weekly parent summary with one coaching focus

---

## Questions to Explore

- Can athletes self-manage more of practice?
- Can force plate testing become self-guided?
- How can video become part of every sprint rep?
- How should parent communication differ for highly engaged parents?

---

## Biggest Lesson

Today's biggest limitation wasn't coaching.

It was administration.

Every interruption reduced the amount of coaching I could provide.
