## Athlete Intelligence

Long-term vision:

Teach athletes to understand their own movement profile.

The goal is not only better coaches.

The goal is smarter athletes capable of making informed training decisions.

## Coaching Intelligence

Long-term vision:

Expand AIP beyond performance training.

Capture expert coaching knowledge from sport-specific coaches and organize it into decision engines.

Examples:

- Defensive Line
- Quarterback
- Offensive Line
- Soccer Skills
- Basketball Shooting
- Baseball Pitching

The goal is to help coaches think like experts while preserving their own coaching philosophy.
