# Smartphone Testing

## Core Principle

AIP must be valuable with only a smartphone.

Advanced tools such as force plates and timing gates can improve insight, but they cannot be required.

---

## Why

Most coaches, parents, schools, and small facilities will not have access to expensive testing equipment.

They will have access to a smartphone.

The product must be built around the tools most coaches already own.

---

## Smartphone Vertical Jump

The smartphone vertical jump test should become one of AIP's first scalable diagnostic tools.

It should help estimate:

- Jump height
- Flight time
- Countermovement depth
- Takeoff position
- Landing position
- Joint angles
- Movement strategy
- Possible limitations

---

## Product Position

The smartphone jump test should not be positioned as a perfect force plate replacement.

It should be positioned as a practical movement screen that gives coaches useful insight without expensive equipment.

---

## Validation Strategy

Force plate data will be used internally as a validation tool.

The goal is to compare smartphone-derived jump metrics against force plate data to determine whether the phone can identify similar movement strengths, weaknesses, and limitations.

---

## Guiding Question

Can we give coaches useful movement insight using only the device in their pocket?
