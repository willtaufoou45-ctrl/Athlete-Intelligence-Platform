# WI-001 — Athlete Flow

## Observation

The live capture workflow follows athlete flow and running order. Repeatedly
searching a long athlete list interrupts that flow.

## Evidence

During sprint capture with 20+ athletes and multiple trials, scrolling to find
the next athlete created the same friction as using Google Sheets and pulled
attention away from coaching.

## Why It Matters

The coach needs to move from one athlete to the next without interrupting the
pace of the session or shifting attention from coaching to navigation.

## Product Implications

Live capture should preserve a predefined athlete order or queue and make the
next athlete immediately available.
