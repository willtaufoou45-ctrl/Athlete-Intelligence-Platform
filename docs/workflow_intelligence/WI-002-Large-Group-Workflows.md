# WI-002 — Large Group Workflows

## Observation

Large recurring teams are divided into smaller recurring training groups, and
manual roster creation does not scale to those environments.

## Evidence

Summit Football has 70+ athletes. Coaches frequently split one team into
recurring groups such as Sprint Group A and Sprint Group B. Recreating or
manually entering those rosters creates significant setup friction.

## Why It Matters

Roster administration grows with group size and delays the start of training.
Recurring subgroup assignments are part of the coach's operating workflow, not
a one-time exception.

## Product Implications

Large rosters should be imported once, organized into persistent subgroups, and
reused across sessions.
