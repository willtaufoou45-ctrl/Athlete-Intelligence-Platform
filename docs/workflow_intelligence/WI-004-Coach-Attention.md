# WI-004 — Coach Attention

## Observation

Administrative interaction during training competes directly with the coach's
attention.

## Evidence

Scrolling to find athletes pulled attention away from coaching. In the June 30
field notes, a parent recorded sprint times so the coach could focus on coaching
instead of handling the timing remote, phone, and data recording simultaneously.

## Why It Matters

Every avoidable navigation or data-entry step reduces the attention available
for athlete feedback and session management.

## Product Implications

Live workflows should minimize searching, repeated setup, and manual data
organization while keeping the next coaching action clear.
