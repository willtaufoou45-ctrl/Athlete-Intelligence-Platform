# Coach Journey
The Coach Journey describes how a coach interacts with ACE during every practice, regardless of the sport, workout, or phase of training.

**Status:** Draft

**Owner:** Will Taufoou

**Purpose**

Describe the ideal coaching experience from the moment a coach opens the platform until they leave practice.

This document intentionally ignores implementation details and focuses on the coach's experience.

---

# Core Principle

The coach's job is to coach.

The platform's job is to remove every administrative interruption while providing the right information at the right time.

# Success Criteria

A successful practice means:

- Coaches spend more time coaching than managing.
- Athletes know what to do without waiting.
- Testing flows naturally.
- Data is captured with minimal manual entry.
- Coaches leave practice knowing exactly what happened.
- Parents understand progress without asking.
- Athletes leave knowing what to improve next.

---

# Vision

When a coach finishes practice they should say:

> "I never had to stop coaching."

When an athlete finishes practice they should say:

> "I know exactly how I performed and what to improve."

When a parent receives the update they should say:

> "I finally understand what my child is working on and why."
